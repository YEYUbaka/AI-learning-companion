# 陕旅版小学英语 四年级上册 知识点

## Unit 1 What is your father
- 词汇：teacher, doctor, nurse, worker, driver, farmer
- 句型：
  - What is your father/mother? — He/She is a...

## Unit 2 On the farm
- 词汇：farm, cow, horse, sheep, goat, chicken, duck
- 句型：
  - What do you have? — I have...

## Unit 3 When do you have classes
- 词汇：get up, go to school, have lunch, go home, go to bed
- 句型：
  - When do you...? — I... at...

## Unit 4 What do you do on Saturday
- 词汇：Saturday, Sunday, play, watch, read, draw
- 句型：
  - What do you do on...? — I...

## Unit 5 Our school
- 词汇：library, playground, office, art room, music room
- 句型：
  - This is our...
  - There is/are... in our school.

## Unit 6 How many pens do you have
- 词汇：how many, pen, pencil, book, bag
- 句型：
  - How many... do you have? — I have...

## Unit 7 I’d like some noodles
- 词汇：noodle, rice, bread, milk, juice, water
- 句型：
  - I’d like some...
  - Would you like some...?

## Unit 8 How much is it
- 词汇：how much, yuan, cheap, expensive
- 句型：
  - How much is it/are they? — It’s/They’re... yuan.
