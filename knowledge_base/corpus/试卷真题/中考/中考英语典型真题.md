# 中考英语典型真题精选

## Part 1: Multiple Choice

### Question 1: Grammar
**Choose the best answer:**
— Have you finished your homework?
— Not yet. I _______ it after dinner.
A. do  B. did  C. will do  D. have done

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:**
- 时间状语"after dinner"表示将来
- 用一般将来时：will do
- A/B是现在/过去时，D是完成时，均不符合

**考点**: 一般将来时

---

### Question 2: Vocabulary
**Choose the word with different pronunciation:**
A. cake  B. face  C. apple  D. make

**Answer:** C

**Explanation:**
- A/B/D中a发[eɪ]音
- C中a发[æ]音
- apple发音不同

**考点**: 元音字母发音规则

---

### Question 3: Phrasal Verb
The meeting was _______ because of the heavy rain.
A. put off  B. put on  C. put up  D. put away

**Answer:** A

**Explanation:**
- put off: 推迟 ✓
- put on: 穿上
- put up: 张贴/搭建
- put away: 收起来
- 因大雨会议被推迟

**考点**: 动词短语辨析

## Part 2: Cloze Test

**Read and choose:**

Last summer, I went to Beijing with my family. It was my __(1)__ time to visit the capital city. We __(2)__ the Great Wall on the first day. It was __(3)__ than I expected. Many tourists __(4)__ photos there.

The next day, we visited the Palace Museum. The buildings were very __(5)__. Our guide told us many interesting __(6)__ about Chinese history. I learned a lot __(7)__ the trip.

1. A. one  B. first  C. once  D. firstly
2. A. visit  B. visits  C. visited  D. visiting
3. A. long  B. longer  C. longest  D. the longest
4. A. take  B. took  C. are taking  D. were taking
5. A. beauty  B. beautiful  C. beautifully  D. more beautiful
6. A. story  B. storys  C. stories  D. story's
7. A. from  B. to  C. with  D. at

**Answers:**
1. B (first time 第一次)
2. C (last summer过去时)
3. B (than提示比较级)
4. D (过去进行时，当时正在拍照)
5. B (were后接形容词)
6. C (many后接可数名词复数)
7. A (learn from 从...学习)

## Part 3: Reading Comprehension

**Passage A**

Tom is a 15-year-old student from London. He loves reading and dreams of becoming a writer. Every day after school, he spends two hours in the library. His favorite books are adventure stories.

Last month, Tom won first prize in a school writing competition. His story was about a boy who traveled around the world. The judges said his story was creative and well-written.

Tom's parents are very proud of him. They encourage him to keep writing. Tom says, "I will work hard to make my dream come true."

**Questions:**

1. How old is Tom?
   A. 13  B. 14  C. 15  D. 16
   **Answer:** C

2. What does Tom want to be?
   A. A teacher  B. A writer  C. A doctor  D. A scientist
   **Answer:** B

3. How long does Tom read every day?
   A. One hour  B. Two hours  C. Three hours  D. Four hours
   **Answer:** B

4. What was Tom's story about?
   A. School life  B. A world trip  C. His family  D. His friends
   **Answer:** B

5. What do Tom's parents do?
   A. Stop him  B. Ignore him  C. Encourage him  D. Punish him
   **Answer:** C

## Part 4: Writing

**Task 1: Complete the dialogue**

A: Excuse me, __(1)__ is the nearest post office?
B: Go straight and __(2)__ left at the second crossing.
A: __(3)__ __(4)__ is it from here?
B: About 10 minutes' walk.
A: Thank you very much.
B: You're __(5)__.

**Answers:**
1. where
2. turn
3. How
4. far
5. welcome
