# 陕旅版小学英语 五年级 知识点

## 五年级上册重点
### Unit 1 My teachers
- 词汇：kind, strict, funny, clever, shy
- 句型：What’s he/she like? — He/She is...

### Unit 2 My week
- 词汇：Monday~Sunday, weekend
- 句型：What do you have/do on...?

### Unit 3 Food
- 句型：What would you like to eat/drink?

### Unit 4 What can you do
- 句型：What can you do? I can... Can you...?

### Unit 5 There is a big bed
- 句型：There is... / There are...

### Unit 6 Nature park
- 句型：Is there...? Are there...?

## 五年级下册重点
### Unit 1 My day
- 句型：When do you...? — I... at...

### Unit 2 Seasons
- 词汇：spring, summer, autumn, winter
- 句型：Which season do you like best?

### Unit 3 Calendar
- 词汇：January~December
- 句型：When is...? It’s in...

### Unit 4 Numbers
- 词汇：first, second, third...
- 句型：When is...? It’s on...

### Unit 5 Whose dog is it
- 词汇：mine, yours, his, hers
- 句型：Whose... is this? — It’s mine.

### Unit 6 Rules
- 句型：Talk quietly. No eating!
