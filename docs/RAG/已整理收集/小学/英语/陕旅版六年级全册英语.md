# 陕旅版小学英语 六年级 知识点

## 核心语法
1. 一般现在时（三单）
2. 一般过去时
3. 一般将来时 be going to
4. There be 句型
5. 人称代词、物主代词
6. 名词单复数

## 六年级上册重点
### Unit 1-2 交通方式
- 句型：How do you go to...? — I go by...

### Unit 3 Weekend plan
- 句型：What are you going to do? — I’m going to...

### Unit 4 Pen pal
- 句型：What are your hobbies? I like...

### Unit 5 Jobs
- 句型：What does he/she do? — He/She is a...

### Unit 6 Feelings
- 句型：How do you feel? You should...

## 六年级下册重点
### Unit 1 打电话
- 句型：May I speak to...? This is... speaking.

### Unit 2 点餐
- 句型：What would you like?

### Unit 3 健康饮食
- 句型：We should eat...

### Unit 4 运动会
- 句型：What are you good at?

### Unit 5 人物描述
- 句型：What’s he/she like?

### Unit 6 暑假计划
- 句型：Where are you going? What are you going to do?
