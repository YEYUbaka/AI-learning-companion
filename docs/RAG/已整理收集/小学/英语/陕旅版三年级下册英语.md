# 陕旅版小学英语 三年级下册 知识点

## Unit 1 My body
- 词汇：head, face, eye, nose, mouth, ear, arm, hand, leg, foot
- 句型：
  - Touch your...
  - This is my...

## Unit 2 My family
- 词汇：father, mother, grandpa, grandma, brother, sister
- 句型：
  - This is my...
  - Who is he/she? — He/She is my...

## Unit 3 How do you come to school
- 词汇：on foot, by bus, by car, by bike, by train
- 句型：
  - How do you come to school? — I come...

## Unit 4 Whose coat is this
- 词汇：coat, shirt, dress, skirt, pants, shoes, whose
- 句型：
  - Whose... is this? — It’s...
  - Whose... are these? — They are...

## Unit 5 Do you like candies
- 词汇：like, candy, apple, banana, orange, grape
- 句型：
  - Do you like...? — Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.

## Unit 6 Where is my eraser
- 词汇：where, in, on, under, near
- 句型：
  - Where is...? — It’s...

## Unit 7 There is a TV in the classroom
- 词汇：there is, there are, classroom, TV, desk, chair
- 句型：
  - There is a...
  - There are...

## Unit 8 The ruler is long
- 词汇：long, short, big, small, tall, fat, thin
- 句型：
  - The... is... / The... are...
