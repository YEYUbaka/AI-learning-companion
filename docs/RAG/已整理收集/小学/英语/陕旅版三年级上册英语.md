# 陕旅版小学英语三年级上册知识点

## Unit 1 Hello
- 词汇: hello, hi, goodbye, bye-bye, name, I, you, he, she
- 句型:
  - Hello/Hi!
  - What’s your name? — My name is...
  - Nice to meet you. — Nice to meet you, too.

## Unit 2 How are you
- 词汇: fine, thank, this, that, is, am, are
- 句型:
  - How are you? — I’m fine, thank you.
  - This is... — Nice to meet you.

## Unit 3 Good morning
- 词汇: morning, afternoon, evening, night, see
- 句型:
  - Good morning/afternoon/evening!
  - Good night!
  - Nice to see you. — Me, too.

## Unit 4 What’s this
- 词汇: this, that, it, cat, egg, pear, apple, bear, panda
- 句型:
  - What’s this/that? — It’s a/an...
  - Is this/that a...? — Yes, it is. / No, it isn’t.

## Unit 5 I have a bag
- 词汇: have, bag, book, pen, pencil, eraser, crayon, ruler
- 句型:
  - I have...
  - Do you have...? — Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.

## Unit 6 Look at my toys
- 词汇: toy, plane, car, doll, ball
- 句型:
  - Look at my...
  - I have... How nice!

## Unit 7 Are these bears
- 词汇: these, those, they, bear, panda, monkey, tiger
- 句型:
  - Are these/those...? — Yes, they are. / No, they aren’t.
  - How many...? — I have...

## Unit 8 What color is it
- 词汇: color, red, blue, green, yellow, black, white
- 句型:
  - What color is it? — It’s...
