# 陕旅版小学英语 四年级下册 知识点

## Unit 1 School Subjects
- 词汇：Chinese, English, math, music, art, PE, science
- 句型：
  - What subject do you like? — I like...

## Unit 2 Feelings
- 词汇：happy, sad, tired, hungry, thirsty, ill
- 句型：
  - How do you feel? — I feel...

## Unit 3 Hobbies
- 词汇：draw, sing, dance, read, swim, play basketball
- 句型：
  - What’s your hobby? — I like doing...

## Unit 4 Daily Routines
- 词汇：brush teeth, wash face, do homework
- 句型：
  - I get up at...
  - He/She gets up at...

## Unit 5 Asking the Way
- 词汇：left, right, straight, turn, hospital, supermarket
- 句型：
  - Where is...? — Go straight. Turn left/right.

## Unit 6 Weather
- 词汇：sunny, rainy, cloudy, windy, snowy, hot, cold
- 句型：
  - What’s the weather like? — It’s...

## Unit 7 My Weekend
- 词汇：weekend, last weekend, visit, play
- 句型：
  - What did you do? — I...
  - Did you...? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn’t.

## Unit 8 Food and Drinks
- 词汇：food, drink, chicken, fish, beef, vegetable
- 句型：
  - What would you like? — I’d like...
